# Personas

One persona for this MVP (`user-stories-questions.md` Q1). Ranked first and only.

## P1 — Riley the used-car shopper

- **Role:** External shopper researching used cars on Car and Driver (`intent-statement.md`)
- **Goals:**
  - See what comparable vehicles list for today, not original MSRP
  - Open that model's current marketplace inventory from the page they are already on
- **Pain points:**
  - Hero, ranking, card backs, and category cards show original MSRP or MSRP ranges
  - That figure does not tell them what cars list for now
- **Context:** Public visitor. No new personal data. Uses current site breakpoints and existing module chrome.
- **Tech comfort:** Medium
- **Frequency:** Occasional research sessions on make/model and category pages
- **Priority:** 1 of 1

No editor, dealer, or logged-in staff persona. Those users are out of this increment.
